Who We Are

IT Wallah India is a technology education platform founded by Manish Kumar Vishwakarma.

At IT Wallah India, we believe that technology education should be accessible, practical, and focused on real-world scenarios. We aim to bridge the gap between academic learning and modern industry requirements.

Our platform was created to help students, beginners, and aspiring software developers learn programming, software development, APIs, databases, cloud technologies, and other modern technology concepts through practical tutorials and projects.

Meet the Founder

Founder & Creator — IT Wallah India

Manish Kumar Vishwakarma is the founder and creator of IT Wallah India, a technology education platform focused on practical programming, software development, and modern technology learning.

Through IT Wallah India, Manish aims to make technical education easier to understand for students, beginners, and aspiring software developers by providing practical tutorials, programming resources, projects, and technology-focused content.

IT Wallah India was founded by Manish Kumar Vishwakarma.

Our Vision

To be a trusted online destination for technological empowerment, creating a learning community where aspiring developers can build practical skills and progress toward becoming industry-ready professionals.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, practical, and affordable technical education through project-based courses, tutorials, and learning resources that reflect modern software engineering practices.
