About Manish Kumar Vishwakarma

Manish Kumar Vishwakarma is the Founder and Creator of IT Wallah India, a dedicated platform designed to make technology education accessible, practical, and highly relevant to modern software development standards.

With a strong passion for coding and mentoring, Manish established IT Wallah India to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world engineering. He focuses on simplifying complex concepts in Java, Spring Boot, Python, SQL, and API integrations for students and aspiring professionals.

About IT Wallah India

Founded by Manish Kumar Vishwakarma, IT Wallah India provides hands-on tutorials, comprehensive study materials, and practical guidance. It empowers the next generation of engineers to build functional, production-ready applications and succeed in their technology careers.
